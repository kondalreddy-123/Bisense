A procurement officer or design engineer may clearly understand product requirements and physical parameters, but navigating thousands of official Indian Standards (IS) without knowing the exact standard number is time-consuming and error-prone.

Inside an Indian Standard

How an Indian Standard is Structured

Every Indian Standard (IS) contains hundreds of critical rules. BISENSE organizes each standard into 6 key categories so you can instantly verify what you need:

🎯
Scope & Purpose Which exact products, ratings, and usage environments are legally covered.
🛡️
Safety Requirements Protection against electric shock, extreme heat, fire hazard, and child safety.
⚡
Performance & Power Rated wattage, energy efficiency limits, mechanical endurance, and durability.
🔬
Testing & Inspection Mandatory lab procedures: high voltage withstand, insulation, and moisture tests.
🏷️
ISI Marking & Labelling Mandatory manufacturer nameplate, batch numbering, and BIS certification emblem.
📊
Sampling & Compliance Official acceptance criteria and factory inspection sampling plans.
